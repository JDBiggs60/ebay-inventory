Old Carpenter eBay Inventory Privacy Policy

Updated October 3, 2026. Operated by James Green, Old Carpenter Workshop.

This app helps its owner organize eBay inventory, item photographs, and storage locations. It is currently being configured for the owner's seller account.

Information used

The app stores its login account information, inventory titles, eBay listing numbers, SKUs, quantities, prices, notes, tote locations, and photographs supplied or imported by the owner. Seller sign-in uses eBay's authorization page. The app does not receive your eBay password. It verifies your eBay account identifier and username, and stores authorization credentials encrypted on its backend. Sign-in state is temporary and expires after ten minutes.

Purpose and service providers

Information is used to save and retrieve inventory, organize totes, and verify the linked seller. GitHub Pages hosts the public app pages. Supabase provides login, database, private photograph storage, and server functions. eBay processes seller authorization and account-deletion notifications. These providers may process technical logs and backups under their own policies. This app does not sell personal information or include advertising trackers.

eBay connection

The current connection verifies the seller account. Automatic listing synchronization is not yet enabled. The current authorization requests basic API access and read-only seller identity access. Inventory imports and tote edits in this app do not themselves change eBay listings.

Retention and deletion

Inventory is retained while it is needed for the owner's use. Verified eBay account-deletion notices are recorded for processing. The configured processor removes the linked seller's eBay inventory records, private inventory photos, and stored authorization credentials when enabled and run. Setup and deletion processing must be completed and checked before live synchronization is enabled. A hashed seller identifier and limited processing records may remain to prevent deleted data from being restored. Provider backups and logs may remain under provider retention policies. User-downloaded backups are outside the server's control; the owner must remove copies containing deleted account data.

Browser storage and access

The browser keeps an app login session and may contain earlier local inventory or downloaded backups. Use a trusted device, sign out on shared devices, and protect exported files. App account credentials and private server credentials must not be published in GitHub.

Contact and control

For access, correction, deletion, or questions about this app, contact Willowchairman@aol.com. You can revoke the application's authorization through your eBay account settings. Revoking access stops future authorized API use; contact the operator to request removal of data already stored.
