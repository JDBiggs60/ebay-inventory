Inventory Problems

These are review prompts based on saved records. A sold record with stock remaining may be a multi-quantity listing or an old physical count.

Unchecked records are not automatically marked missing. Physical checks older than 90 days are flagged for another count.

Backup & Export

A full JSON backup includes every record and all uploaded item photos. Keep a copy on your computer or in your phone's Files app.

CSV is a spreadsheet copy and does not include photos. Use the JSON backup to transfer test records between phone and computer.

Restore a Full Backup

Restore merges records. A matching record is replaced by the backup version; other current records remain. Download a current backup first.

Electronics Inventory backups are rejected to keep your two apps separate.
