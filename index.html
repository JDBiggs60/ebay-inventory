QR Label Privacy Check

An inventory QR is a link to this app, not a password. Private inventory still needs your signed-in account and database access rules. Anyone can read the printed title, SKU, tote and item number on a label.

  1. Make an inventory label for a test item.
  2. Scan it using a different phone that is signed out, or copy its link into a private browser window.
  3. Confirm that the smiley-face thank-you page appears and no private records or photos are visible. The small Owner sign-in button should open the login form.
  4. For a label that may ship to a buyer, choose Public eBay listing or Text only when making it.

A QR link cannot grant access by itself. This checklist is a manual test, not proof that database permissions have been audited.
